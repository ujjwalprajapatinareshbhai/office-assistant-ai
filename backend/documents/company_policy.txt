Company Policy

Working Hours:
Employees are expected to work from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday.

Attendance:
Employees must mark attendance before 9:15 AM.

Leave Policy:
Employees receive 20 paid leaves every year.

Confidentiality:
Company data must not be shared outside the organization.

Internet Usage:
Office internet should only be used for work-related purposes.

Dress Code:
Employees should wear business casual attire.

Remote Work:
Employees may work remotely with manager approval.
